Designed against silent failure

The workflow stays inspectable before generation gets expensive.

BarelyWorks makes upstream mistakes, missing evidence, stale handoffs, and provider failures visible instead of reporting a false pass.

01

Versioned artifacts

New revisions never silently replace the active Head, provenance, or approval history.

02

Cumulative verification

Later stages recheck every applicable upstream artifact, contract, hash, and approval.

03

Human approval gates

Approval, provider submission, rough cuts, and final delivery require explicit actions.

Local-first boundaries
